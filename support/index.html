Getting started

Open Nit Boost, click the sun icon in the macOS menu bar, and enable XDR boost. When boosting is active, use Boost strength to adjust the effect.

Compatibility

Nit Boost requires a compatible Mac display that reports extended dynamic range headroom. If no XDR headroom is available, the boost control will be unavailable.

Power and temperature

Higher display brightness may increase power consumption and device temperature. Available brightness can vary with the Mac model, display, content, power conditions, and temperature.
